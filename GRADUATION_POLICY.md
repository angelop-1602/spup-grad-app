# Graduation Application Policy

## 1. "No Application, No Graduation" Policy

The **"No Application, No Graduation"** policy is strictly enforced. Submitting an official graduation application is a **mandatory requirement** as it serves as the basis for evaluating a student's academic record and eligibility for graduation. Students who fail to submit an application within the designated window period will not be eligible for graduation.

## 2. Application Windows

- Application windows are time-bound periods established by the Registrar's Office
- Each window is identified by **Month and Year** (e.g., "March 2026")
- Applications must be submitted within the active window period
- Late submissions will not be accepted after the window end date

## 3. How to Apply for Graduation

### Step 1: Complete & Submit the Form Online
- Fill out the Graduation Application Form with accurate and complete information through the online system
- If you are a student residing outside the region, coordinate with your **Adviser or Program Coordinator** for assistance
- Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly
- Keep track of application deadlines

### Step 2: Verification & Payment
After submitting your online application:
- **Claim your verified form** at the Registrar's Office
- **Proceed to the Dean's Office** for approval
- **Settle your graduation fee** at the Finance Office

### Step 3: Final Submission
- Submit the **completed and signed form** to the Registrar's Office to finalize your application

## 4. Application Requirements

### Basic Information Required
- Personal information (name, student ID, contact details)
- Complete educational background (Elementary through Graduate Studies)
- Graduation appearance preference (attending/not attending)
- Degree/Title applying for (Department, Course, Major)

### Graduate Program Additional Requirements
For **Master's and Doctoral** programs:
- Subject enrollments (up to 6 subjects with code, title, and units)
- Thesis/Dissertation Title
- Thesis/Dissertation Adviser

### Confirmation
- Agree to comply with the requirements for the degree
- Confirm your Student ID number

## 5. Application Status

Applications progress through the following statuses:
- **Submitted**: Application submitted online
- **Under Review**: Being reviewed by coordinator
- **Approved**: Approved by coordinator (after verification and payment)
- **Incomplete**: Requires additional information or documents
- **Rejected**: Application rejected

### Application Editing
- Students may edit applications only while status is "Submitted"
- Once status moves to "Under Review" or beyond, editing is not allowed
- Students can view and download their application form at any time

## 6. Requirements Checklist

Coordinators verify the following requirements:

1. **Entry Requirements**: Transcript of Records (TOR) / Form 137, PSA Birth Certificate / Marriage Certificate
2. **Complete Grades**: All course grades must be complete and verified
3. **Hardbound Copies**: Theses/Dissertation Books with CDs (for graduate programs)
4. **ID Picture**: 2x2 ID Picture with white background and nametag
5. **Reviewer Certification**: Signed by In-Charge of Language Editing (for graduate programs)
6. **Journal Publication**: Certificate signed by CPRINT Director (for graduate programs)

## 7. Student Responsibilities

- Keep profile information current and accurate
- Submit applications within the active window period
- Complete all requirements
- Monitor application status using Student Number
- Complete verification and payment process after online submission
- Respond promptly to requests for additional information

## 8. Support and Contact

- **Registrar's Office**: 09155648291

- For technical support, contact the Registrar's Office immediately

## 9. Important Notes

- Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly
- Keep track of application deadlines
- Applications can only be edited while status is "Submitted"

- Complete the verification and payment process promptly after online submission
